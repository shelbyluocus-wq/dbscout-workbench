## Made for Blackcat

Four pictures (and their `thumbnails/`) were produced for Blackcat with an AI
image generator, then resized to 2048×1152 and encoded as WebP. They are not
Cindy files.

- mist-ridges, night-ridges: generated from text descriptions only.
- frost-folds: generated with Cindy's indigo-folds supplied as a visual
  reference, as its light counterpart.
- dune-night: generated with Cindy's dune-dawn supplied as a visual reference,
  as its dark counterpart.

The two reference-based pictures follow the composition of Apache 2.0 licensed
Cindy artwork; the notice above and `LICENSE-Cindy.txt` cover that origin.

Blackcat's Vue wallpaper controls, local storage and canvas are an independent
implementation informed by Cindy's wallpaper design. No Cindy character artwork,
branding, video loops, account integration or CDN service is included.
